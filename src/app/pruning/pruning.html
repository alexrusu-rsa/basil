Pruning

Start pruning when the basil plant is about 6 inches tall. Use clean, sharp scissors or pruning shears to avoid damaging the plant. Pinch or cut just above a leaf node (where leaves grow from the stem) to encourage bushier growth. Regularly prune the top leaves to prevent the plant from flowering, which can cause the leaves to become bitter. Harvest leaves as needed, but avoid removing more than one-third of the plant at a time to ensure healthy regrowth.
