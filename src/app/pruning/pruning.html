Pruning

hourglass

When to start

Start pruning when the basil plant is about 15 cm tall. content_cut

Tools

Use clean, sharp scissors or pruning shears to avoid damaging the plant. spa

Technique

Pinch or cut just above a leaf node (where leaves grow from the stem) to encourage bushier growth. macro_off

Prevent flowering

Regularly prune the top leaves to prevent the plant from flowering, which can cause the leaves to become bitter. eco

Harvesting

Harvest leaves as needed, but avoid removing more than one-third of the plant at a time to ensure healthy regrowth.
